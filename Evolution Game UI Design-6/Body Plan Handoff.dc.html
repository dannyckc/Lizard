THE ONE RULE OF THIS SYSTEM

A creature is one authored resource (CreatureParams) — bones, weights and angles. Everything else is derived per tick: no preset carries a gait, a footfall order, a stride length, a height, or a “bipedal” flag. An Elephant ambles because its knees do not fold; a T. rex is bipedal because its arms measure too short to bear. Implement the derivations, not the outcomes.

01

The Body

AUTHORED · CreatureParams.gd

The body is a spine chain of verlet particles (point 0 is the head and the driver) wearing a width profile: knots sampled head → tail with a Catmull-Rom spline. Mass is never a parameter — it is the drawn silhouette’s volume × density × surviving tissue, so a broader body is a heavier body and a chewed-open one is lighter.

02

The Skeletal Structure

DERIVED · BodyPlan.gd

BodyPlan is the only file that names a body part. The animal is a tissue lattice: one body patch of 32 × 7 cells (8 head-cap + 20 torso + 4 tail-cap columns), plus four limb patches of 10 × 3 (6 bone + 4 foot columns). Rows are odd so one row straddles the spine and carries the vertebral column and the cord inside it. The skeleton is sparse by design — a flood fill from the snout must reach every bone cell, and a bite landing in a rib gap reaches the muscle the ribs do not cover.

03

Posture & Articulation

Posture.gd · Articulation via CreatureParams

One trait — the angle the limbs are carried out of the ground plane — and everything else is read off it. plan_reach = limb × cos(tilt) (footprint from above), clearance = limb × sin(tilt) (the animal’s height — there is no height number), track = plan_reach × cos(tilt) (how far outboard the feet fall). Posture is an anatomical category, not a tuning group — and it never says how many legs the animal walks on.

BIPEDALISM IS A MEASUREMENT

arm_length ≥ leg_length × BEARING_RATIO (0.46) → the forelimbs bear. Below it they are carried, folded against the chest, and duty, footfall and weight are quoted against two legs. No bipedal flag exists anywhere.

04

The Gait

DERIVED · Footfall.gd + Locomotion.gd

Every terrestrial gait is three numbers, re-derived each tick — no gait is a mode, preset, or name anything checks for:

girdle_lag
How far the fore girdle follows the hind. 0 = pace, 0.25 = lateral-sequence walk, 0.5 = trot, 0.60 = gallop (forelimbs land late — the suspension)
hind_split
Phase between the two hind limbs. 0.5 = alternating; collapses toward 0.06 as the animal goes asymmetric — hind pair first (the half-bound)
fore_split
Same for the forelimbs; collapses as aerial². Sign vs hind_split under a turn = rotary vs transverse gallop

What decides them — four measured inputs: Froude (v²/g·hip — the regime; the only place speed enters), launch (sin(tilt) × spine_freedom × leap capability — can a girdle throw the body), caution (1 at a walk: must be statically supported), and interference (would same-side feet collide — why a camel paces). Phases: RL = 0, RR = hind_split, FL = girdle_lag, FR = girdle_lag + fore_split.

The one hard law: a body cannot outrun its own legs. leg_speed = foot travel / hurried cycle is a ceiling on the body; move_speed is what the species asks for and gets the lower of the two. The only authored coordination traits are foot_lead (0.45 — how far ahead feet aim) and beat_coupling (0.55 — how tightly partners keep a shared beat), plus rest-stance biases (+0.30 front / −0.25 rear). Lay-low is not a gait: it is a crouch spent out of hind_fold_range at STALK_SPEED 0.40 of walk.

Implementation Notes

  1. Build in derivation order: params schema → posture table lookup → lattice from silhouette + girdle t’s → physique (mass off the drawn volume) → locomotion (duty, swing, stride, leg_speed ceiling) → footfall (the three numbers, per tick) → animation reads phases.
  2. Refresh order matters: Locomotion refreshes once per tick from the previous tick’s physique (deliberate one-tick lag); Footfall.update runs after the body is solved, taking hip height, speed, reach, girdle gap, lead and spring.
  3. Nothing selects an outcome. If an implementation needs a “gait state” enum, an “is bipedal” flag, or a per-species stride/step/leap number, it has diverged from this spec — those were all removed from the source for causing exactly the bugs their comments describe.
  4. Footfall is a scorer, not a driver: a foot must already be due (drifted past its stride) before the pattern picks which of the due feet goes first (off_beat). Two limbs within 0.12 of a cycle are one beat; two closer than swing_share may be aloft together.
  5. Kangaroo slow walk — design recommendation: the HUD draws it pentapedal (tail + arms as the fifth limb); the sim currently does a plain alternating stride. Flagged for implementation, not yet in source.
